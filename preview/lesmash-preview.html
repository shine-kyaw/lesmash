Privacy

This notice explains how Le SMASH Burgers & Co. handles information on this website.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies for advertising or tracking. If you choose a branch, that choice is stored in your own browser so the site can remember it. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

Analytics

We measure how the site is used in aggregate — which pages are opened, and which buttons are pressed — so we can keep it fast and useful. This measurement is cookieless and does not identify you individually.

Third parties

We load no social media scripts, embeds or advertising pixels. Links to Facebook, Instagram, Google Maps and Foodpanda are ordinary links: nothing is sent to those companies until you choose to follow one, at which point their own privacy terms apply.

Ordering

Delivery orders are placed on Foodpanda, not here. Any name, address or payment detail you enter is handled by Foodpanda under their terms. We never see it.

Contact

For any question about this notice, please contact the restaurant using the details on the locations page.
